## Custom-question answer style (Wellfound and similar)

- Simple, straight, first-person sentences. No `--`, no em dash, no emoji, no markdown, no bullet lists, no "I am a fresher".
- 2–4 sentences, roughly 250–360 characters. It should read like a human typed it in a minute.
- Tie 1–2 concrete facts from that job description to 1–2 resume/project facts. Read the JD first; never reuse a generic answer.
- Do not overclaim. If the JD asks for a technology not on the resume, do not say you have used it; talk about the closest real experience.
- Avoid salary, notice period, or personal details in a custom answer unless the question asks for them.

Example shape:
> I like that <company> builds <specific thing from the JD>. I have worked with <stack> and <project fact> in my own projects, and this role lets me use those skills while learning <domain from the JD> at a growing company.

## Formatting conventions for Notion

- **Salary:** exactly as posted. `₹6,00,000/yr`, `₹25,000/mo`, `₹1,500/day`, or `Not listed`. Do not convert currencies or invent a number.
- **YOE Required:** `Fresher`, `0-1 yr`, `0-2 yr`, `1+ yr`, or `Not stated`. Use `2+ yr` only when the user explicitly overrides the fresher gate. Otherwise the most specific number the JD gave.
- **Status:** `Applied` on creation. Later: `In Progress`, `Interview`, `Offer`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`.
